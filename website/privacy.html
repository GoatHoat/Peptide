Progress notes and photographs

This is the most sensitive category in the app, and it is named here rather than covered by a general phrase about "your content".

If you add a progress note, Pepstack stores the text, any body measurement you enter, and — if you attach one — a photograph you took, which may be a photograph of your own body.

Photographs are held in a private Supabase Storage bucket, in a folder belonging to your account, access-controlled so that only requests authenticated as you can read them. They are not public, they are not indexed, and they are not sent to the AI assistant or to any third party. They are deleted when you delete the note, and when you delete your account.

2. What Pepstack does not collect

This list is true as of the date at the top. It stops being true the moment an analytics package is added, and this policy would change first.

  • No location, of any precision.
  • No contacts, calendar, microphone, or access to your photo library beyond a single photo you deliberately attach.
  • No advertising identifier and no device fingerprint.
  • No analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.
  • No tracking of you across other apps or websites.
  • Your personal data is never sold, and never shared for advertising.
  • No health data is read from or written to Apple Health.

3. Lawful bases, and why some of this is special

Some of what Pepstack holds is special-category health data under Article 9 of the UK and EU GDPR: your menstrual status, your dietary restrictions, your health goals, the supplements you take, your adherence history, the reasons you skipped a dose, the notes you write about how you react to something, and your progress notes and photographs.

The lawful basis for processing that data is your explicit consent, given when you provide it — not legitimate interest. You may withdraw it at any time by deleting the data or your account, and every one of those fields is optional.

For everything else:

  • Contract — your email, password and subscription tier, because there is no account without them.
  • Legitimate interest — the message count used to rate-limit the assistant, and reported answers, so the service can be kept working and safe.

4. Who else sees it

Three processors. Each does one job.

Supabase

Database, authentication and file storage — everything in section 1. Row-level security is enabled on every table holding your data, so a request authenticated as you can only reach rows belonging to you. Traffic is encrypted in transit, and Supabase encrypts data at rest. The project is hosted in [TO CONFIRM: Supabase project region]. [TO CONFIRM: whether that region means UK/EU data leaves the region, and if so which transfer safeguard applies]

Anthropic

Only when you send a message to the AI assistant, and only what that message needs. Your message goes to a server function, which adds context and calls Anthropic's API.

What is sent: your question, the recent conversation, and context loaded on the server — your age, sex, dietary restrictions, reported reactions, goals, current stack, schedule and adherence. It also includes any notes you have written about how you react to something, and the short summary the assistant made of them, so it does not ask you the same thing twice. A note you delete under You is not sent again.

What is not sent: your email address, your display name, your account identifier, and your progress notes or photographs.

It is used to answer that request. If you never open the assistant, nothing is ever sent to Anthropic.

Apple

If subscriptions are offered, purchases are handled by Apple through in-app purchase. Apple processes the payment and holds the receipt; Pepstack receives only whether your account is subscribed. 6. Your rights

If you are in the UK, the EU, or another region with equivalent law, you have the rights below. Most are exercisable in the app without contacting anybody.

  • Access and portability — You → Export Data produces everything you have entered, as CSV or PDF.
  • Correction — every answer can be changed in the app at any time.
  • Erasure — You → Delete Account. Permanent, immediate, and it does not require emailing anybody.
  • Withdrawing consent — every sensitive field is optional; clearing it withdraws consent for it.
  • Objection and restriction — write to the contact address above.

You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority. [TO CONFIRM: the supervisory authority for the country of establishment]

# ILLUSTRATIONS.md

Complete illustration inventory, screen by screen.

**Two registers only.**
- **LINE** — technical line art. Single-weight copper stroke, no fill. The system
  default. Everything below is LINE unless marked.
- **OBJECT** — rendered pill/vial with real specular highlight. Used *only* for
  the physical thing a user actually swallows. Never for icons, never for
  decoration.
- **CLOUD** — point-cloud convergence. Celebration moments only. The particles
  always resolve into a LINE form, so the two registers are one system.

**The whole list is 22 unique assets.** Several appear on multiple screens in
different states, which is deliberate — a reused object with a changed state
reads as a system, two different drawings read as clip art.

---

## THE TWO RECURRING OBJECTS

These carry the app. Get these two right before drawing anything else.

**① THE ORGANISER** — a weekly 7-compartment pill box, drawn in three-quarter
isometric, lid hinged open. Appears empty, filling, and full.

**② THE VESSEL** — a graduated measuring cylinder with an etched MAX line.
Appears overflowing, comfortably under, and dropping below the line. This is the
app's signature image: it is the upper-limit idea made physical.

### The eight goal glyphs

Lean anatomical here — it pulls toward the engraved-plate register and makes the
set feel like a reference work rather than an icon pack.

| Goal | Subject |
|---|---|
| Sleep | Crescent moon, thin |
| Recovery | A wave returning to a baseline, with the baseline marked |
| Energy | A rising curve breaking a horizon line |
| Focus | Concentric rings converging to a point — an aperture closing |
| Training | Dumbbell, three-quarter isometric |
| General health | Anatomical heart, engraved line style |
| Skin & hair | A hair follicle in cross-section, skin layers visible |
| Gut | Coiled intestine, anatomical plate style |

The follicle and the gut are the two that will make people notice this set. Do
not swap them for a face and a stomach — the technical specificity is the point.

## CELEBRATIONS — CLOUD, resolving into LINE

Five moments only. Each resolves into a **different** form so it is never the
same trick twice.

| Moment | Resolves into |
|---|---|
| Onboarding complete | ① The organiser, filling compartment by compartment |
| First 7-day streak | Seven dots forming a row, then a closed ring |
| 30 days | A calendar month grid, drawn line by line |
| 100 days | The numeral 100, engraved-weight |
| **First time under the ceiling** | ② The vessel — fill *dropping* below the MAX line |
| Year in review | A full year ring, 365 ticks |

The under-the-ceiling one is the most important moment in the product. They came
in at 45mg, they changed something, they're at 32. That is the app doing the only
thing it exists to do, and it resolves into the same vessel they saw on their
very first audit screen. Nothing else in the app should feel bigger than this.

## WHERE NOT TO PUT ILLUSTRATION

Deliberate absences. Each of these is a screen where art would make things worse.

- **Any data-dense screen.** Today, Analysis totals, adherence, cost. A chart is
  already an illustration.
- **Profile rows.** Icons next to settings labels are noise. Text alone.
- **The audit's over-limit tiles.** They invert. That is the whole event. An icon
  inside would dilute it.
- **The schedule reveal.** The generated timetable *is* the payoff.
- **The tab bar.** Standard SF Symbols. This is not where you express identity.
